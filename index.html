The Meaning

Why GADELA?

GADELA means Money Hunter in Ewé, a Togo local language. GA means Money and ADELA means Hunter. So the combination of GA and ADELA gives you GADELA.

Wearing GADELA motivates and reminds you every day that you gotta hustle and make shit happen out the hood.
